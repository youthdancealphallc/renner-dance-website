Always Accepting Applications

Three Key Roles at Renner Dance

We review applications year-round for three roles and make room for standout candidates even after a season's schedule is set.

Operations & Family Experience

Studio Operations

You keep the studio running day to day: greeting families, coordinating schedules and events, and supporting teachers and parents.

What You Will Own
  • Warm front-desk hospitality and day-to-day parent communication
  • Class registration, billing support, and scheduling in Dance Studio Pro
  • Studio readiness, lobby organization, and smooth class transitions
  • Event, recital, summer camp, and competition logistics coordination
Who thrives here: Organized, warm, proactive communicators who enjoy working with families and running a busy studio behind the scenes.
Apply for This Role →
Foundations & Joy · Ages 2 to 18

Recreational Dance Teacher

Teach engaging, technically sound weekly classes in Ballet, Jazz, Tap, Hip Hop, Acro, Lyrical, Contemporary, or Preschool Combo in a joyful, structured classroom.

What You Will Own
  • Age-appropriate lesson planning with healthy technical foundations
  • Building confidence, rhythm, teamwork, and joy in every student
  • Choreographing memorable, age-appropriate annual recital pieces
  • Communicating warmly with parents and celebrating student milestones
Who thrives here: Energetic, patient, caring dance educators who make foundational training both disciplined and fun.
Apply for This Role →
Company & Pre-Professional · Elite Training

Advanced & Company Faculty

Train, choreograph for, and mentor our competitive Company and pre-professional Ballet Academy dancers, bringing conservatory-level rigor, artistic vision, and real industry experience.

What You Will Own
  • High-level technique classes across Ballet, Contemporary, Jazz, Lyrical, Tap, Hip Hop, or Acro
  • Original, award-caliber competitive choreography and rehearsal cleaning
  • Coaching dancers for regional/national conventions and collegiate auditions
  • Mentoring serious young artists in discipline, artistry, and resilience
Who thrives here: Accomplished choreographers, conservatory-trained educators, and working or former professionals who pair high standards with real care.
