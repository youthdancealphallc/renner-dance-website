Our Story

How Renner Dance Began

At Renner Dance, movement is more than technique; it is a legacy. Our founder and artistic director, Summer Renner Disher, grew up immersed in a family where athletic discipline and high-level performance were a way of life.

Her father, Bill Renner, played in the NFL with the Green Bay Packers before becoming a respected high school football coach, while her brother, Bryn Renner, set records as a standout quarterback at UNC and played in the NFL before transitioning into coaching.

Summer carved her own distinguished path in dance. She trained under the legendary Miss Mary Day at The Washington Ballet, earned her BFA in Dance from Florida State University where she captained the FSU Golden Girls to a 2nd place national ranking at UDA Nationals, and performed for six seasons as a Radio City Rockette in New York City. Certified through the ABT Teacher Training program and currently serving as Head Coach of the UNC Carolina Girls Dance Team, Summer founded Renner Dance in Chapel Hill in 2015 to blend the athletic rigor of sports with the expressive artistry and joy of dance.

At Renner Dance, we believe in nurturing versatile, resilient dancers who lead with confidence both on and off the stage, values deeply rooted in Summer’s family legacy.

“We welcome families who seek a dance education that fosters growth, confidence, and a lifelong love for movement.”

The People Behind the Magic

Our Team

Under the artistic leadership of Founder & Artistic Director Summer Renner Disher, our faculty brings extraordinary professional experience, genuine care for students, and a shared commitment to developing every dancer’s potential.

Program Directors & Senior Company Faculty

Our program directors and senior faculty focus their time primarily on training and mentoring our competitive Company teams and pre-professional Ballet Academy dancers, bringing decades of international conservatory pedigree, national choreography honors, and technical leadership.

Rossana Nesta
Ballet Mistress · Academy Director
Classical Ballet Cecchetti Vaganova

Trained at Balletto di Roma and certified at the Accademia Nazionale di Danza in Rome, Ms. Rossana has taught and choreographed internationally for over 30 years. Her students have gone on to top-tier companies and schools such as the New York City Ballet, American Ballet Theatre, Royal Ballet London, Finnish National Ballet, and more. She brings a legacy of elite training and mentorship to every student she teaches.

Mady Jackson
Company Director · Excel, Extreme & Edge
Lyrical Jazz Tap

Mady has been teaching dance across the country for over 10 years and has been with Renner Dance since the very beginning. She loves teaching a variety of styles but especially enjoys lyrical, jazz, and tap.

Her students have earned numerous honors, including choreography and entertainment awards, and have been named National Champions at various competitions and conventions. One of her proudest moments was receiving a Critics' Choice Award for a tap routine at NYCDA.

For the past eight seasons, Mady has also been deeply involved in the competition and convention circuit, teaching, judging, and connecting with dancers from around the world. This season, she is stepping into a directorial role at the studio, leading the Excel, Extreme, and Edge companies.

She is excited to bring her knowledge and experience into the studio and help take all Renner dancers to the next level, whether it's their first year dancing or their eleventh.

Rachel Carr
Ballet Academy Assistant Director
Ballet Pointe Conditioning

Graduate of Point Park University (B.A. Dance Performance). As Assistant Director of the Ballet Academy, Ms. Rachel plays a key role in classical instruction, choreography, variations coaching, and program coordination alongside Ms. Rossana.

Angelina Diaz
Contemporary · Jazz · Ballet · Choreographer
Contemporary Choreographer YoungArts Winner

A dancer and choreographer raised in Miami, FL and recently relocated to Raleigh, NC, Angelina graduated from Miami Dance Collective in 2020 and received the prestigious National YoungArts Foundation Merit Award in Choreography before furthering her training at SUNY Purchase and Springboard Danse. She currently tours and teaches with Adrenaline and Revive Dance Conventions, showcased her work at Open Doors North America 2024, was named DDCF 2024 Rising Choreographer, and was a finalist for The Dance Educators Collective in Nashville 2025. Recently featured in an interdisciplinary National YoungArts Foundation production under Mario Alberto Zambrano, she will premiere her new work “The Weight of A Soft Goodbye” with DanceCity Festival in New York City and Chicago in 2026.

Chaveli Peña
Ballet · Contemporary · Lyrical
Classical Ballet Contemporary Lyrical

Chaveli Peña is a passionate and dedicated dance artist and instructor with a strong foundation in classical ballet, contemporary, and modern dance techniques. She received her formal training at prestigious institutions including the International Ballet Academy, the Magda Auñon School of Classical Ballet, and most recently, the School of Carolina Ballet, where she spent two years in their pre-professional division before being promoted to an apprentice with the professional company.

Throughout her training, Chaveli has performed an extensive range of classical and contemporary repertoire, including lead roles in Giselle, The Nutcracker, Don Quixote, and La Fille Mal Gardée, as well as works by renowned choreographer Zalman Raffael during her time with Carolina Ballet.

Chaveli's performance experience is complemented by a deep love for teaching. She has coached competition solos and variations since 2021 and served as the primary ballet teacher at Dance Ascension in Garner, NC from 2023–2024, where she also taught jazz, lyrical, and contemporary. In the summer of 2024, she returned to Carolina Ballet to assist with their summer intensive program.

Known for her technical precision, musicality, and ability to nurture creativity in young dancers, Chaveli brings both artistry and encouragement into the studio. She is excited to continue inspiring the next generation of dancers through disciplined training and a heartfelt love for the art of movement.

Karen Perez
Acro · Dance · Cheer
Acro Specialist Choreographer 20+ Years Teaching

Karen Perez is a graduate of the University of Maryland, where she earned a Bachelor of Arts degree in Dance and Choreography. During her time at Maryland, she was a member of the UMD Cheerleading Squad and performed at events such as the Gator Bowl and the Women's Final Four.

Karen began her dance training at the young age of five and never looked back. Throughout her dance career, she has performed in numerous productions, including works at Western Connecticut State University, Dean College, and the University of Maryland. She has been teaching dance and acro for over 20 years and has previously directed several dance programs, working with both competitive and recreational students. Her choreography for solos and group routines has received numerous awards.

In addition to dance, Karen has coached all-star cheer and dance teams as well as high school dance programs. Acro is her specialty, and she has developed curricula for multiple studios in the area. Beyond her extensive dance experience, Karen has also taught preschool and has a deep passion for working with children.

Karen lives in Clayton, NC, with her husband and three children.

Ally Organo
Ballet · Jazz · Contemporary · Hip Hop · Tap · Acro
UNC Carolina Girls UDA Nationals Multi-Style

Ally Organo, originally from Apex, NC, began dancing at just two years old. She spent five years competing with the Academy for the Performing Arts' Elite program, where she trained extensively in ballet, jazz, contemporary, hip-hop, tap, acrobatics, and more. In addition to her studio training, Ally was a leading member of Apex High School's Dance Company, performing in numerous concerts and taking part in various choreography opportunities throughout the school year. After graduating, she earned a coveted spot on the Carolina Girls Dance Team at UNC-Chapel Hill, where she performs at home football and men's basketball games and competes at the UDA Collegiate National Championship. Ally is deeply passionate about dance and is dedicated to sharing that passion with all her students.
