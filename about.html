Our Story

How Renner Dance Began

At Renner Dance, movement is more than technique; it is a legacy. Our founder, Summer Renner Disher, grew up immersed in a family where athleticism and performance were a way of life.

Her father, Bill Renner, was a punter in the NFL with the Green Bay Packers and later became a respected high school football coach, known for his discipline and strategic mindset. Her brother, Bryn Renner, followed suit as a standout quarterback at the University of North Carolina, setting records for passing accuracy and touchdowns before playing in the NFL and transitioning into coaching.

Summer carved her own path in the world of movement. After performing as a Radio City Rockette and coaching the UNC Dance Team, she founded Renner Dance in Chapel Hill, NC. Her vision was to create a studio that blends the athletic discipline of sports with the expressive artistry of dance.

At Renner Dance, we believe in nurturing versatile dancers who are as resilient as they are graceful. Our training emphasizes strong technique, adaptability, and a positive mindset — values deeply rooted in Summer’s family legacy.

"We welcome families who seek a dance education that fosters growth, confidence, and a lifelong love for movement."

Radio City Rockette UNC Dance Team Coach Founder & Artistic Director Chapel Hill, NC

What Drives Us

Mission & Culture

Our Mission

Beyond
the Steps

Dance is our medium, but our mission goes far beyond the steps.

At Renner Dance Company, we are committed to helping young people grow into strong, resilient, disciplined, and compassionate individuals.

Through the rigor of dance training, our students learn to embrace failure, build patience, and grow confidence in the face of challenge. They learn how to collaborate and coexist with peers, how to lead and how to follow, how to push through discomfort, and how to take pride in showing up every day, even when it is hard. We do not just teach technique; we help shape character. Our goal is to help every dancer leave the studio not only more skilled, but also more mature, grounded, and ready to thrive in life beyond the stage.

Our Culture

Excellence,
Versatility,
Respect

A culture of excellence, versatility, and respect — on and off the stage.

Our training philosophy is rooted in high standards and individualized growth. We teach to the level of our most advanced dancers, creating an environment where ambitious students are constantly challenged and those still developing are inspired to rise. This dynamic fuels rapid progress, technical mastery, and a shared sense of purpose in every class.

Versatility is a cornerstone of our curriculum. We train dancers across a wide range of styles and techniques, preparing them to be adaptable, confident performers in any setting.

Competitions are an important part of our dancers’ development. They offer opportunities to apply their training, perform under pressure, and grow as both artists and teammates. Our company dancers participate in a core set of competitions that support their technical and artistic journey. We also encourage and celebrate those who choose to explore additional competitive opportunities across the country, as long as they carry our values with them. At Renner Dance, success is not measured by trophies. It is measured by how dancers grow, how they lead, and how they show up for themselves and others.
