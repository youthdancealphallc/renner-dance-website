Our Story

How Renner Dance Began

At Renner Dance, movement is more than technique; it is a legacy. Our founder and artistic director, Summer Renner Disher, grew up immersed in a family where athletic discipline and high-level performance were a way of life.

Her father, Bill Renner, played in the NFL with the Green Bay Packers before becoming a respected high school football coach, while her brother, Bryn Renner, set records as a standout quarterback at UNC and played in the NFL before transitioning into coaching.

Summer carved her own distinguished path in dance. She trained under the legendary Miss Mary Day at The Washington Ballet, earned her BFA in Dance from Florida State University where she captained the FSU Golden Girls to a 2nd place national ranking at UDA Nationals, and performed for six seasons as a Radio City Rockette in New York City. Certified through the ABT Teacher Training program and currently serving as Head Coach of the UNC Carolina Girls Dance Team, Summer founded Renner Dance in Chapel Hill in 2015 to blend the athletic rigor of sports with the expressive artistry and joy of dance.

At Renner Dance, we believe in nurturing versatile, resilient dancers who lead with confidence both on and off the stage, values deeply rooted in Summer’s family legacy.

“We welcome families who seek a dance education that fosters growth, confidence, and a lifelong love for movement.”

What Drives Us

Mission & Culture

Dance is our medium, but character is our mission. Every class is designed so your child leaves our studio not only more skilled, but stronger, more grounded, and ready to thrive far beyond the stage.

Our Mission · Beyond the Steps

We Don’t Just Teach Technique. We Shape Character.

Four life skills built in every class
01

Resilience

Students learn to embrace mistakes, push through discomfort, and discover that real growth happens when things feel challenging.

02

Discipline & Grit

Dancers build patience, focus, and pride in showing up every day prepared to work, even when it is hard.

03

Teamwork & Leadership

In the studio, young people learn how to collaborate with peers, how to lead and how to follow, and how to lift teammates up.

04

Grounded Confidence

Every dancer leaves our doors more mature, poised, and self-assured, carrying quiet confidence into school and life.
