How It Works

Three Simple Steps.

1
Create an account on Dance Studio Pro
DSP is our registration platform. Create a free account at app.gostudiopro.com/online/rennerdance. It takes about two minutes.
2
Sign up for a free trial class
Browse the class schedule, pick the style and time that works for your dancer, and register for a free trial online with no payment required.
3
Come dance with no pressure and no obligation
Show up, meet the teachers, and experience Renner Dance for yourself. Enroll only if your dancer loves it.
