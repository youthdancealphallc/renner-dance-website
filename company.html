From Weekly Classes to a Team Experience

How Company Works

In recreational dance, families choose individual classes one by one and perform in a year-end recital. Stepping into Company means joining a dedicated team built around four distinct pillars that work together to develop complete, confident performers.

01 Classroom Training

Fixed Multi-Style Curriculum

Instead of picking individual classes à la carte, each team follows a structured weekly curriculum across core disciplines including Ballet, Jazz, Tap, Lyrical, Contemporary, Hip Hop, Acro, and Musical Theatre. Training across complementary styles eliminates technical gaps and builds strong, versatile dancers.

Ballet · Jazz · Tap · Lyrical · Contemporary · Hip Hop · Acro · Musical Theatre
02 Ensemble Performance

Team Dances & Rehearsals

Separate from their weekly technique classes, dancers rehearse original group choreography with their teammates. Working together to master formations, musicality, and storytelling teaches shared accountability and gives dancers the pride of representing their team as one unit.

Original Choreography · Precision Formations · Team Camaraderie
03 Individualized Spotlight

Solos, Duets & Trios

In addition to team dances, eligible Company dancers have the opportunity to perform custom solos, duets, and trios. Working one-on-one or in a small group with our faculty allows dancers to showcase their unique strengths, receive personalized mentorship, and build commanding stage presence.

One-on-One Coaching · Custom Routines · Personal Artistry
04 Stage & Industry Events

Competitions & Conventions

Rather than waiting all year for a single recital, Company dancers take the stage multiple times each spring at regional and national competitions for professional adjudication, and attend conventions where they train in master classes led by nationally renowned choreographers.

Professional Adjudication · National Stages · Convention Master Classes
National Convention & Competition Stages 24 Seven Dance Convention
Award-Winning Group Choreography Onstage New York

Why Families Make the Leap

What Company Does for Your Child

Parents often tell us that joining Company was the moment their child truly blossomed, not just as a dancer, but as a young person. Here is why this next step makes such a lasting difference.

Accelerated Progress

A Year of Growth in Months

When a child dances once a week, much of each class is spent reviewing from the week before. In Company, consistent multi-style training surrounded by motivated peers sparks rapid breakthroughs in strength, flexibility, coordination, and artistry that your child will feel proud of right away.

Real-World Poise

Confidence Under the Spotlight

Stepping onto a stage in front of judges and audiences, whether with teammates or in a solo, teaches children how to channel nerves into focus, apply constructive feedback with grace, and carry themselves with self-assurance in school, interviews, and life.

Positive Peer Community

A Second Family That Lifts Them Up

Who your child spends time with matters. Company surrounds your dancer with hardworking, encouraging teammates and caring mentors. Dancers travel together, cheer for one another backstage, and build deep friendships rooted in shared effort and mutual respect.

Future Opportunities

Doors Opened to College & Beyond

Company training builds the time management, grit, and technical pedigree that colleges and directors look for. Our alumni have earned merit scholarships, spots on Division I dance teams including the UNC Carolina Girls and FSU Golden Girls, and professional performance careers.

Poise, Grace & Classical Foundation Stage Performance
Confidence & Camaraderie Under the Lights Company Showcase

Four Progressive Teams

Find the Right Fit

Dancers are placed by audition into one of four progressive teams based on age, skill, and commitment, ensuring every child is challenged at the right pace with a clear pathway to advance in future seasons.

Entry Level
Excel Company
A supportive first step from recreational classes into competitive team dance

The foundational level of Renner Dance Company, ideal for young and developing dancers ready to try competitive dance with a manageable weekly schedule. Excel dancers build core multi-style technique while experiencing team choreography and local competitions for the first time.

Commitment 1 required day/week · 1 optional additional day
Events 3 local competitions
Team Dances Performs 1 group routine
Curriculum Ballet, Jazz, Tap, Lyrical, Contemporary
Placement by Audition Inquire About Excel
Intermediate
Extreme Company
Building strength, versatility, and individual stage presence

A step up in training intensity and performance opportunities. Extreme dancers deepen their technical foundation across styles, attend their first dance convention, and become eligible for custom solo, duet, and trio routines.

Commitment 1–2 required days/week + optional days
Events 3 local competitions + 1 in-state convention
Team Dances Performs 1–2 group routines
Spotlight Eligible for solo, duet & trio opportunities
Curriculum Ballet, Jazz, Tap, Lyrical, Contemporary
Placement by Audition Inquire About Extreme
Advanced
Edge Company
Sharper technique, bigger stages, and expanded style versatility

An advanced competitive team for dedicated dancers ready for higher technical and artistic demands. Edge dancers train across an expanded curriculum, perform in multiple group routines plus optional solos, duets, or trios, and travel to regional competitions and conventions.

Commitment 2 required days/week + optional days
Events 3–4 competitions + conventions (some with travel)
Team Dances Performs 2 or more group routines
Spotlight Eligible for solo, duet & trio opportunities
Curriculum Ballet, Jazz, Tap, Hip Hop, Lyrical, Contemporary
Placement by Audition Inquire About Edge
Our Highest Level
Elite Company
Pre-professional training for dancers aspiring to college teams and professional careers

Renner Dance Company's top competitive team for our most dedicated and accomplished dancers. Elite dancers train at a pre-professional level with access to guest master classes, intensives, custom solos/duets/trios, and a premier national competition and convention schedule.

Commitment 2+ required days/week
Events 4+ conventions + competitions; guest intensives
Team Dances 2 guaranteed group routines + additional routines by audition
Spotlight Eligible for solo, duet & trio opportunities
Curriculum Ballet, Jazz, Tap, Hip Hop, Musical Theatre, Lyrical, Contemporary
Specialty Track May audition for the studio's Pre-Pro Tap program
Placement by Audition Inquire About Elite
