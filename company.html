Competitive Track

Company
Programs.

Renner Dance Company is our competitive team program. It offers a fixed weekly curriculum across Ballet, Jazz, Tap, Lyrical, Contemporary, Hip Hop, Acro, and Musical Theatre for dancers who want structured multi-style training and team performance opportunities.

From Weekly Classes to a Team Experience

How Competitive Dance Works

RDC… Count On Me.

If your family is familiar with recreational dance, where students sign up for individual classes one by one and perform in a year-end recital, our Company Program is the next step. Company dancers train together as a dedicated team, build well-rounded technique through a set curriculum, and represent Renner Dance on stage throughout the season, preparing them to dance in college, on a collegiate dance team, or professionally.

📚

Fixed Weekly Curriculum

Rather than choosing individual classes à la carte, each Company team follows a structured weekly curriculum of required technique classes across styles such as Ballet, Jazz, Tap, Lyrical, Contemporary, Hip Hop, Acro, and Musical Theatre.

💃

Team Choreography

Alongside their weekly technique classes, dancers rehearse group routines with their teammates. As dancers advance through our teams, they also gain opportunities to perform in multiple group routines as well as solos, duets, and trios.

🏆

Competitions & Conventions

During the spring season, teams attend regional and national dance competitions to perform on stage and receive feedback from professional judges, as well as dance conventions where they take master classes from top industry faculty.

R.D.C. Core Values

The Renner Way

RDC… Count On Me. It is more than a motto. It is a standard we hold ourselves to every day as dancers, as teammates, and as people.

R
Respect

Conducting oneself with integrity, honesty, courtesy, and consideration for teammates and faculty. Being someone your team can count on.

Also: Responsibility. In every interaction, on every stage, and in every hallway of this studio.

D
Driven

Determination to achieve personal goals while leading by example. Bringing full effort to every class and every event, even when it is uncomfortable.

The grind is the point. Showing up when it is hard is exactly when it matters most.

C
Character

Personal integrity, leadership, and professionalism in the studio and beyond. Supporting and encouraging teammates and representing the studio well in every interaction.

Who you are when no one is watching is who you are as a Renner Dance Company dancer.

RDC dancers are encouraged to celebrate each other's strengths, support one another through challenges, and see themselves as part of one unified team, not competitors within their own studio.

Joining Company is a Commitment

What We Expect

Our standards are high, because we believe in your dancer's potential.

📅

Full Attendance

Full attendance to all assigned classes and rehearsals. Consistency is foundational to a dancer's growth and to the team's success.

🕐

Punctuality & Preparation

Arrive on time, in dress code, and ready to work. Respect for your own time and your teammates' time is non-negotiable.

👍

Positive Conduct

Positive, respectful conduct in and out of the studio. You represent Renner Dance everywhere you go.

💬

Open Communication

Proactive communication about conflicts or absences. We work together as a team, and keeping us informed helps everyone succeed.

📋

Studio Policies

Adherence to studio policies and dress code. Structure and standards exist to set every dancer up for success.

🌟

Team-First Mindset

Support your teammates and celebrate their growth as genuinely as your own. A rising tide lifts all boats.

Four Progressive Teams

Find the Right Fit

Dancers are placed by audition into one of four progressive teams based on age, skill, and commitment, with a supportive team environment and a clear pathway to advance into higher levels in future seasons.

Entry Level
Excel Company
A supportive first step into the world of competitive dance

The foundational level of Renner Dance Company, introducing young and developing dancers to competitive team training in a supportive environment. Excel dancers build core technique across styles while getting their first taste of team culture, choreography, and the competition experience.

📅 Commitment: 1 required day/week · 1 optional additional day
🏆 Events: 3 local competitions
💃 Routines: Performs 1 group dance
📚 Curriculum: Ballet, Jazz, Tap, Lyrical, Contemporary
Placement by Audition Inquire About Excel
Intermediate
Extreme Company
Building strength, versatility, and stage presence

A step up in training intensity and commitment, Extreme dancers deepen their technical foundation across Ballet, Jazz, Tap, Lyrical, and Contemporary while expanding their performance and convention experience as part of the RDC team.

📅 Commitment: 1–2 required days/week + optional days
🏆 Events: 3 local competitions + 1 in-state convention
💃 Routines: Performs 1–2 group dances
📚 Curriculum: Ballet, Jazz, Tap, Lyrical, Contemporary
⭐ Eligible for solo, duet & trio opportunities
Placement by Audition Inquire About Extreme
Advanced
Edge Company
Sharper technique, bigger stages, deeper commitment

An advanced competitive team for dancers ready for a greater challenge. Edge holds dancers to a high standard of attendance, professionalism, and class conduct, including competitions and conventions that may require travel, preparing them for the step up to Elite.

📅 Commitment: 2 required days/week + optional days
🏆 Events: 3–4 competitions + conventions (some with travel)
💃 Routines: Performs 2 or more group dances
📚 Curriculum: Ballet, Jazz, Tap, Hip Hop, Lyrical, Contemporary
⭐ Eligible for solo, duet & trio opportunities
Placement by Audition Inquire About Edge
Our Highest Level
Elite Company
For dancers pursuing dance at the highest level

Renner Dance Company's top competitive team, for the studio's most dedicated and advanced dancers. Elite dancers train at a pre-professional level with access to guest master classes, intensives, and a rigorous competition and convention season across Ballet, Jazz, Contemporary, Lyrical, Tap, Hip Hop, Acro, and Musical Theatre.

📅 Commitment: 2+ required days/week
🏆 Events: 4+ conventions + competitions; guest intensives
💃 Routines: 2 guaranteed group dances + additional routines by audition
📚 Curriculum: Ballet, Jazz, Tap, Hip Hop, Musical Theatre, Lyrical, Contemporary
⭐ Eligible for solo, duet & trio opportunities
🩰 May audition for the studio's Pre-Pro Tap program
Placement by Audition Inquire About Elite
