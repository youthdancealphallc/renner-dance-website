Company
Programs.

Renner Dance Company is our competitive team program — four progressive levels of training across Ballet, Jazz, Tap, Lyrical/Contemporary, Hip Hop, Acro, and Musical Theatre. Whether your dancer is just stepping into competition or training toward a professional career, there is a level built for where they are right now.

Audition-Based Placement · All Levels

Four Levels. One Vision.

RDC… Count On Me.

Renner Dance Company exists to help dancers achieve new levels of artistry and performance beyond what's possible in regular class work — training and empowering students to a pre-professional level with a competitive edge, whether they go on to dance in college, on a dance team, or professionally.

Our Company dancers are grouped into four progressive levels based on age, skill, and commitment. Each level offers structured training, performance opportunities, a supportive team atmosphere, and a clear pathway for advancement. Dancers audition into the level that best fits where they are right now — with a path to grow into higher levels in future seasons.

📋
Auditions — All Company Levels

All four Company programs (Excel, Extreme, Edge, and Elite) require an audition. Auditions take place every May for the following dance year. Auditions evaluate dancers across Ballet, Jazz, and Lyrical/Contemporary technique, plus improvisation. Interested families should contact us in advance to receive audition details and preparation guidelines.

R.D.C. Core Values

The Renner Way

RDC… Count On Me. It is more than a motto. It is a standard we hold ourselves to every day — as dancers, as teammates, and as people.

R
Respect

Conducting oneself with integrity, honesty, courtesy, and consideration for teammates and faculty. Being someone your team can count on.

Also: Responsibility. In every interaction, on every stage, and in every hallway of this studio.

D
Driven

Determination to achieve personal goals while leading by example. Bringing full effort to every class and every event, even when it is uncomfortable.

The grind is the point. Showing up when it is hard is exactly when it matters most.

C
Character

Personal integrity, leadership, and professionalism in the studio and beyond. Supporting and encouraging teammates and representing the studio well in every interaction.

Who you are when no one is watching is who you are as a Renner Dance Company dancer.

RDC dancers are encouraged to celebrate each other's strengths, support one another through challenges, and see themselves as part of one unified team — not competitors within their own studio.

Joining Company is a Commitment

What We Expect

Our standards are high, because we believe in your dancer's potential.

📅

Full Attendance

Full attendance to all assigned classes and rehearsals. Consistency is foundational to a dancer's growth and to the team's success.

🕐

Punctuality & Preparation

Arrive on time, in dress code, and ready to work. Respect for your own time and your teammates' time is non-negotiable.

👍

Positive Conduct

Positive, respectful conduct in and out of the studio. You represent Renner Dance everywhere you go.

💬

Open Communication

Proactive communication about conflicts or absences. We work together as a team -- keeping us informed helps everyone succeed.

📋

Studio Policies

Adherence to studio policies and dress code. Structure and standards exist to set every dancer up for success.

🌟

Team-First Mindset

Support your teammates and celebrate their growth as genuinely as your own. A rising tide lifts all boats.

The Levels

Find the Right Fit

Each level is designed for dancers at a specific stage of their journey. Placement is determined by our staff.

Entry Level
Excel Company
A supportive first step into the world of competitive dance

The foundational level of Renner Dance Company, introducing young and developing dancers to competitive team training in a supportive environment. Excel dancers build core technique across styles while getting their first taste of team culture, choreography, and the competition experience.

📅 Required: 1 day/week · Optional: 1 additional day
🏆 Attends 3 local competitions
💃 Performs 1 group dance
📚 Ballet, Jazz, Tap, Lyrical/Contemporary
Audition Required · Placements in MayInquire About Excel
Intermediate
Extreme Company
Building strength, versatility, and stage presence

A step up in training intensity and commitment, Extreme dancers deepen their technical foundation across Ballet, Jazz, Tap, Lyrical/Contemporary, and more, while building performance and competition experience as part of the RDC team.

📅 Required: 1–2 days/week + optional days
🏆 3 local competitions + 1 in-state convention
💃 1–2 group dances
📚 Ballet, Jazz, Tap, Lyrical/Contemporary
⭐ Eligible for solo, duet & trio opportunities
Audition Required · Placements in MayInquire About Extreme
Advanced
Edge Company
Sharper technique, bigger stages, deeper commitment

A mid-level competitive team for dancers ready for a greater challenge. Edge holds dancers to a high standard of attendance, professionalism, and class conduct — including competitions and conventions that may require travel — preparing them for the step up to Elite.

📅 Required: 2 days/week + optional days
🏆 3–4 events + conventions (some with travel)
💃 2 or more group dances
📚 Ballet, Jazz, Tap, Hip Hop, Lyrical/Contemporary
⭐ Eligible for solo, duet & trio opportunities
Audition Required · Placements in MayInquire About Edge
Our Highest Level
Elite Company
For dancers pursuing dance at the highest level

Renner Dance Company's top competitive team, for the studio's most dedicated and advanced dancers. Elite dancers train at a pre-professional level with access to guest master classes, intensives, and a rigorous competition and convention season across Ballet, Jazz, Contemporary, Lyrical, Tap, Hip Hop, and Musical Theatre.

📅 Required: 2+ days/week
🏆 4+ conventions + competitions; guest intensives
💃 2 guaranteed group dances + more by audition
📚 Ballet, Jazz, Tap, Hip Hop, Musical Theatre, Lyrical/Contemporary
⭐ Eligible for solo, duet & trio opportunities
🩰 May audition for the studio's Pre-Pro Tap program
Audition Required · Placements in May Inquire About Elite
