Our Class Offerings

Our Class Offerings.

Explore all our class offerings. Each style is taught with passion, technique, and care by our experienced faculty.

Ballet

Ballet is the foundation of all dance forms, focusing on technique, posture, alignment, and grace. It develops discipline, strength, and musicality, and is essential training for dancers of all levels and styles.

Our Teaching Approach At Renner Dance, ballet is taught with a strong emphasis on both technical precision and artistry — within an environment that values encouragement, effort, and safe progression. We promote healthy technique while helping each dancer build strength, confidence, and focus.
Try a Ballet Class Register Now

Jazz

Jazz dance is high-energy and dynamic, characterized by sharp lines, rhythmic footwork, and stylistic flair. It combines technique with performance quality and is often featured in musical theatre and commercial dance.

Our Teaching Approach Jazz classes at Renner emphasize strong foundational technique, dynamic movement, and musicality. Students learn combinations that challenge their strength, precision, and performance presence — always in a fun and engaging setting.
Try a Jazz Class Register Now

Tap

Tap dance is a rhythmic and percussive style where dancers use their feet like instruments. It develops musicality, timing, and coordination through intricate footwork and sound patterns.

Our Teaching Approach Our tap program is built on clarity of sound, musical phrasing, and creativity. We create a positive, upbeat space where dancers of all ages can explore rhythm and expression while learning foundational to advanced tap vocabulary.
Try a Tap Class Register Now

Hip Hop

Hip hop is a high-energy street style rooted in rhythm, freestyle, and individuality. It emphasizes groove, attitude, and self-expression, and often features styles like popping, locking, and breaking.

Our Teaching Approach We provide a supportive, high-energy space where students can build confidence, learn foundational moves, and enjoy the freedom to express themselves. Technique, musicality, and clean execution are reinforced at every level.
Try a Hip Hop Class Register Now

Contemporary

Contemporary dance blends elements of ballet, jazz, and modern technique to explore fluidity, emotional expression, and musical interpretation. It encourages individuality and personal storytelling through movement.

Our Teaching Approach In contemporary, we focus on helping dancers find their unique voice while building strong technique. Our instructors promote exploration, emotional depth, and a respect for musical nuance — all within a safe, fun, and technically sound environment.
Try a Contemporary Class Register Now

Acro

Acro dance combines classical dance technique with athletic acrobatic elements. It emphasizes flexibility, strength, balance, and coordination. Dancers learn to seamlessly integrate tricks and tumbling into choreography.

Our Teaching Approach Our acro classes prioritize proper body mechanics, safe spotting, and confidence-building. Students are encouraged to progress at their own pace while developing the strength and flexibility required for more advanced skills.
Try an Acro Class Register Now

Lyrical

Lyrical dance blends elements of ballet and jazz to create emotive, expressive movement that interprets music and lyrics. It's graceful and fluid, often telling a story or conveying feeling through dance.

Our Teaching Approach In lyrical, we focus on connection — to the music, the story, and the audience. Dancers learn to balance emotion with control, strength with softness, and technique with artistry — all in a nurturing and encouraging environment.
Try a Lyrical Class Register Now

Combo Classes

Designed for our youngest dancers, Combo classes introduce multiple styles in a single, fun-filled session. Typical combinations include Tap / Ballet / Tumble and Ballet / Jazz — giving little ones a well-rounded taste of movement, rhythm, and expression all in one class.

Our Teaching Approach Combo classes are taught in a nurturing, playful environment built for short attention spans and big imaginations. Each session rotates between styles to keep energy high and learning joyful — a perfect first step into the world of dance.
