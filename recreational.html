Styles We Teach

Our Class Offerings

Every style is taught with technical care, creativity, and joy by experienced faculty.

Ballet

The foundation of all dance forms, ballet builds technique, posture, alignment, strength, and musicality for dancers of every style.

Our Teaching Approach We balance technical precision with artistry and progress dancers safely, building confidence and focus through encouragement.
Try a Ballet Class View Schedule & Register

Jazz

High-energy and dynamic, jazz features sharp lines, rhythmic footwork, and stylistic flair, and is common in musical theatre and commercial dance.

Our Teaching Approach Classes emphasize foundational technique and musicality. Students learn combinations that build strength, precision, and stage presence.
Try a Jazz Class View Schedule & Register

Tap

A percussive style where dancers use their feet as instruments, building musicality, timing, and coordination.

Our Teaching Approach We focus on clear sounds, musical phrasing, and creativity, teaching tap vocabulary from foundational to advanced in an upbeat class.
Try a Tap Class View Schedule & Register

Hip Hop

A high-energy street style rooted in rhythm, groove, and self-expression, often featuring popping, locking, and breaking.

Our Teaching Approach Students learn foundational moves and build confidence and personal style, with technique, musicality, and clean execution reinforced at every level.
Try a Hip Hop Class View Schedule & Register

Acro

Acro combines dance technique with acrobatics, building flexibility, strength, and balance as dancers learn to blend tricks and tumbling into choreography.

Our Teaching Approach Safety comes first: proper body mechanics, careful spotting, and progress at each dancer's own pace toward advanced skills.
Try an Acro Class View Schedule & Register

Lyrical

Lyrical blends ballet and jazz into fluid, expressive movement that interprets the music and lyrics, often telling a story.

Our Teaching Approach We focus on connection to the music, the story, and the audience, teaching dancers to balance emotion with control and technique with artistry.
Try a Lyrical Class View Schedule & Register

Combo Classes

Designed for our youngest dancers, Combo classes introduce several styles in one session. Typical combinations include Tap / Ballet / Tumble and Ballet / Jazz.

Our Teaching Approach Playful classes built for short attention spans and big imaginations. Rotating between styles keeps energy high, making Combo a great first step into dance.
Try a Combo Class View Schedule & Register
