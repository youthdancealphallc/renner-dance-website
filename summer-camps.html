Common Questions

Camp FAQs

Do campers need prior dance experience?

No prior dance experience is required. Our summer camps welcome complete beginners as well as returning dancers.

What should my child wear to camp?

Form-fitting clothes (leggings or dance shorts and a fitted top) with hair pulled back. Dance shoes are ideal but not required on day one. We'll send details when you register.

What is the cancellation policy?

Registrations cancelled more than 14 days before the camp start date receive a full credit toward another camp week or class registration. Cancellations within 14 days are non-refundable but may be transferred to another week or another dancer in the same household.

Will there be a performance at the end of the week?

Yes. Every camp week ends with a mini showcase on Friday afternoon where campers perform what they learned. Family and friends are invited.

Are the camp spots limited?

Yes. Each week has capped enrollment so every dancer gets proper attention. We recommend registering early.

How do I register?

Through Dance Studio Pro, our online registration and payment platform. Click any "Register on Dance Studio Pro" button on this page.
