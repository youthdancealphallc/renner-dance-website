Common Questions

Camp FAQs

Do campers need prior dance experience?

No experience is required for any of our camp weeks (except the Junior Company Intensive, which recommends at least 1 year of dance experience). Our camps are welcoming to first-time dancers and returning students alike.

What should my child wear to camp?

Comfortable, form-fitting clothing is best — leggings or dance shorts, a fitted top, and hair pulled back away from the face. Dance shoes are ideal but not required for the first day. We'll send specific guidance when you register.

What is the cancellation policy?

Registrations cancelled more than 14 days before the camp start date receive a full credit toward another camp week or class registration. Cancellations within 14 days are non-refundable but may be transferred to another week or another dancer in the same household.

Will there be a performance at the end of the week?

Yes! Every camp week ends with a mini showcase on Friday afternoon where campers perform the choreography they learned during the week. Family and friends are warmly invited to attend.

Are the camp spots limited?

Yes — each camp week has a maximum enrollment to ensure every dancer receives proper attention and instruction. We recommend registering early to secure your child's spot.

How do I register?

All camp registration is done through Dance Studio Pro, our online registration and payment platform. Click any "Register on Dance Studio Pro" button on this page to get started.
