Quick Answers

What ages do you accept? +

We welcome students from age 2 through 18+ in our recreational program. Company and Ballet Academy programs have specific age and skill requirements.

Do I need prior experience? +

No experience is required for recreational classes. We welcome beginners at all ages. Company and Ballet Academy placement is based on skill evaluation.

Can we try a class first? +

Absolutely. We offer a free trial class for all new students. Use the "Try a Free Class" button anywhere on the site to sign up.
