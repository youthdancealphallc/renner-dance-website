Backed by Science

Why Every Child
Should Dance.

Dance is one of the most powerful activities a child can pursue. The research is clear, and the results go far beyond the stage.

The Whole Child

More Than Movement

Parents often think of dance as a creative outlet or a physical activity. It's both, and the research shows it's also one of the most effective tools for developing your child's brain, body, and character.

Studies published in Frontiers in Psychology, the New England Journal of Medicine, and by leading researchers at Harvard confirm that dance engages the motor cortex, cerebellum, and hippocampus simultaneously, providing a "full-brain workout" unlike almost any other childhood activity.

Research-Backed Benefits

What Dance Does for Your Child

Every benefit below is grounded in published research. Dance is not just an art form; it's one of the most complete developmental tools available to children.

Cognitive Development

Brain Growth & Working Memory

Learning and performing choreography activates the hippocampus, motor cortex, and prefrontal cortex simultaneously. Research confirms dancers show faster cognitive processing, stronger working memory, and sharper academic focus.

Frontiers in Psychology · Harvard Medical School
Auditory & Academic Transfer

Musicality & Pattern Recognition

Internalizing beat, phrasing, and tempo strengthens auditory processing and rhythmic synchronization, skills directly linked to stronger performance in reading, mathematics, and language arts.

NYU · National Endowment for the Arts
Physical Mastery

Coordination & Low-Impact Fitness

Dance builds cardiovascular endurance, core strength, balance, and flexibility without the injury rates of contact sports, supporting healthy posture and joint stability as children grow.

American Heart Association · ACSM · CDC
Personal Growth

Confidence & Composure

Rehearsing and performing builds self-confidence rooted in earned mastery. Stage experience teaches children how to channel nerves into focus and carry themselves with poise under pressure.

University of Maryland · Santa Monica College
Community & Empathy

Social Skills & Teamwork

Ensemble classes develop cooperation, trust, active listening, and shared accountability, creating lasting friendships and a genuine sense of belonging.

Ashokan Center · CASEL
Character & Artistry

Discipline & Creative Expression

Consistent practice and constructive feedback cultivate resilience and work ethic, while creative movement gives children a healthy vocabulary for self-expression and emotional regulation.

US Dept. of Education Arts Research
Confidence & Poise on Stage Classical Performance
Teamwork & Shared Accomplishment Ensemble Performance

Beyond the Studio

Dance Opens Doors.

For dancers who choose to pursue their training seriously, dance becomes more than a passion; it becomes a competitive advantage. Top colleges and universities actively recruit talented dancers, and merit-based scholarships are available at hundreds of institutions.

College Dance Scholarships

Hundreds of accredited colleges and universities, from major research universities to conservatories, offer merit-based scholarships for dance. Renner Dance alumni have earned scholarships to the University of Arizona, Pace University, Florida State University, and more.

Collegiate Dance Teams

Programs like the UNC Carolina Girls, FSU Dance Team, and similar high-profile collegiate teams actively recruit technically trained dancers. Strong studio training is the primary differentiator in these auditions.

Pre-Professional & Conservatory Programs

Competitive dancers have a clear pathway into some of America's most prestigious pre-professional programs, ballet academies, and performing arts conservatories, opening doors to professional company careers.

Professional Careers in Dance & Entertainment

Renner Dance alumni have performed for Mariah Carey, choreographed for the Backstreet Boys, performed at the FIFA World Cup, and appeared in major films. A strong technical foundation opens opportunities that go far beyond traditional dance careers.
